## 4. Canadian Tax Advantages & Government Incentives

### 4.1 SR&ED (Scientific Research & Experimental Development)

**The single most valuable tax incentive for Canadian tech startups.**

| Metric | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **What it covers** | Salaries of developers doing experimental development (new features, new architecture) |
| **Federal Credit** | 35% refundable credit on first $800K of qualifying R&D expenditures (CCPCs under $500K taxable income) |
| **Ontario Credit** | Additional 3.5% non-refundable |
| **Effective Rate** | Up to 64.3% combined return on eligible R&D salaries (CCPC enhanced rate) |
| **Year 1 Eligible Spend** | ~$192,000 (developer + CTO R&D time: ~80% of technical salaries) |
| **Year 1 Credit** | ~$67,000-$90,000 (pending claim complexity) |
| **Year 2 Eligible Spend** | ~$400,000 |
| **Year 2 Credit** | ~$140,000-$180,000 |

**Questy-specific SR&ED eligible activities:**
- Building the gamification engine adaptations for B2B channel workflows (new use case)
- Multi-tenant isolation improvements and enterprise auth system
- AI-driven task recommendation engine
- Deal registration workflow with conditional logic
- Partner tier auto-qualification algorithms

### 4.2 NRC IRAP (Industrial Research Assistance Program)

| Metric | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **What it is** | Non-repayable grants for innovative SMEs (\<500 employees, \<$50M revenue) |
| **Typical grant** | $50,000-$150,000 per project |
| **Covers** | Salary costs for R&D personnel |
| **Timeline** | 3-6 month application, 6-12 month project |
| **Questy fit** | Strong — B2B SaaS pivot with novel gamification application |

### 4.3 Ontario Innovation Tax Credit (OITC)

| Metric | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Rate** | 8% non-refundable credit on qualifying R&D expenditures |
| **Max** | $300,000 per year |
| **Stackable** | Yes, stacks with SRED |

### 4.4 Ontario Interactive Digital Media Tax Credit (OIDMTC)

| Metric | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Rate** | 40% refundable credit on eligible labour + marketing (for interactive digital media products) |
| **Max** | No annual cap |
| **Questy fit** | Potentially eligible — gamified platform qualifies as interactive digital media. Requires Ontario Certified Product ruling. |
| **Estimated Year 1** | $30,000-$50,000 if eligible |

### 4.5 Canada Digital Adoption Program (CDAP)

Relevant for Questy's customers, not Questy itself — but important for GTM: Canadian SMEs receive up to $15,000 in grants to adopt digital tools. Questy can position as a CDAP-eligible solution.

### 4.6 Total Government Incentive Stack (Year 1 Estimate)

| Program | Estimated Return (CAD) |
|---------|----------------------|
| SR&ED Federal | $67,000-$90,000 |
| SR&ED Ontario (Provincial) | $7,000-$10,000 |
